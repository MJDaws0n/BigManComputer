Privacy Policy

Last updated: 01/03/2026

1. Introduction

Big Man Computer ("BMC", "we", "us") takes your privacy seriously. This policy covers what data we collect, why, how it's used, and your rights under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Data Controller

The data controller for BMC is MJ Dawson. For any data protection queries, please contact via mjdawson.net or direcly via email at contact@mjdawson.net.

3. What Data We Collect

We collect the minimum amount of data necessary to provide our service:

  • Account information: Username and hashed password (if you create an account). Passwords are salted and hashed, we never store them in plaintext.
  • Saved programs: BMC source code you choose to save (if logged in).
  • Consent records: Timestamp of when you agreed to this privacy policy.
  • Session data: A session token cookie to keep you logged in.
  • Human verification (AutoGate captcha): If you use the Log In or Sign Up forms, we use AutoGate to help prevent abuse. This involves loading AutoGate's client script from autogate.mjdawson.net and sending your captcha token to our server, which then validates it with AutoGate. AutoGate may process your IP address and related technical data as part of this verification (see their terms: autogate.mjdawson.net/tos).

4. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect email addresses.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • We do not log or store IP addresses for user activity (though AutoGate may process IP data during captcha verification on login/signup, see their terms).
  • We do not use advertising or marketing trackers.
  • We do not collect any special category data.

5. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under Article 6 of UK GDPR, we process your data based on:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): You consent to data processing when you create an account and agree to this policy.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): Essential cookies are used solely for authentication functionality.

6. Cookies

We use only essential cookies required for authentication:

No tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies are used. As these are strictly necessary cookies, they are exempt from cookie consent requirements under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), but we still let you know about them for transparency.

7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access (Article 15): You can export all your data at any time from the Dashboard.
  • Right to erasure (Article 17): You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from the Dashboard. This is immediate and irreversible.
  • Right to data portability (Article 20): You can download all your data in JSON format from the Dashboard.
  • Right to withdraw consent: You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.
  • Right to rectification (Article 16): You can edit or delete your saved programs at any time.

8. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored securely on the server.
  • Passwords are salted and hashed before storage.
  • Session tokens are generated using cryptographic hashing.
  • No data is transmitted to third-party services except for AutoGate captcha validation during login/signup (token validation request to autogate.mjdawson.net).

9. Data Retention

Your data is retained only for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, all associated data (account information, saved programs, session data, and consent records) is permanently and immediately deleted.

10. Children's Privacy

BMC is suitable for users of all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. We only ask for a username and password, which doesn't include any personally identifying information.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or your data, please contact me via the contact form on my website mjdawson.net or directly via email at contact@mjdawson.net.
